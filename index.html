About this model

What it is

The Mobius torus (also spelled Möbius or Moebius torus, also called a twisted torus) is a geometric line drawing: a donut shape with an octagonal opening, covered in rhombus-shaped facets that spiral from the inner opening to the outer rim. It is used as a stained glass pattern, a colouring page and geometric line art. This page turns that flat 2D pattern into an interactive 3D model.

Purpose

To visualise a flat drawing in 3D: to see what the hidden back looks like, and to follow each twisted band as it climbs the front, crosses the rim, travels around the back and comes back through the opening.

What it shows

The front view matches the flat drawing line for line. Each of the 8 crescent bands starts on one side of the octagonal opening and climbs 7 sections to the rim. The back follows the same geometry as the front, so each band makes one full turn around the hole and closes on itself: 8 separate bands, coloured here with 4 colours.

About the name: despite being called a Mobius torus, the surface is not a Möbius strip. A torus always has an inside and an outside; it is the bands on it that are twisted.

The facets that look like rhombs are rectangles seen at an angle. Each corner of the octagonal opening is a short vertical edge on the inner wall, seen end-on, which is why the octagon's sides tilt in depth.

How to use

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Highlight one band to see its centerline (dashed where it passes behind). Choose a colour preset or pick your own colours to try colouring ideas.
